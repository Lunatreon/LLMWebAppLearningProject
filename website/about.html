About Lu

How I became free.

I stopped treating inherited scripts as instructions and began writing a life from emotional honesty, practical design, and repeated choice.

The beginning

FeelY grew from a refusal to live on default settings.

I built my own syllabus from emotional literacy, systems thinking, romantic bravery, and the question: what changes when I stop abandoning my own perception?

The answer was not one dramatic reinvention. It was a series of quieter decisions: noticing what my body knew, taking responsibility without turning it into shame, and designing structures that made honest choices easier to repeat.

FeelY is the place where that work becomes shareable.
